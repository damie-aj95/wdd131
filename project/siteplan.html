Ballers Market — Site Plan

Site Name

Ballers Market

This name reflects the brand's identity as a marketplace for authentic, wearable sneakers, appealing directly to buyers looking to purchase sneakers to wear.

Site Purpose

Ballers Market provides a platform for browsing authentic sneakers, learning how the buying process works, saving favorite pairs, and reaching out to purchase.

Scenarios

  • How do I know the sneakers are authentic?
  • How can I contact someone to buy a pair I like?
  • Can I save sneakers I'm interested in to check later?

Color Schema

  • Black (#0d0d0d): background
  • Green (#2ecc71): headings, buttons, links, accents
  • White (#ffffff): body text, borders

Typography

  • Bebas Neue: headings and navigation
  • Poppins: body text and paragraphs
